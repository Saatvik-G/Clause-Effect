## 🎯 Quick Demo Checklist for Evaluators

1. **Test Decode Mode**:
   - Open app → Click **"Try a sample"** → Choose **"Rental Agreement"**.
   - Click any clause card on the left or highlight on the paper.
   - Click the **"🔊 Read Aloud"** button to hear plain English speech.
   - Drag the **What-If Simulator** slider between Month 2 and Month 6.
   - Click **"Copy Email Draft"** in the Negotiation Helper.

2. **Test Compare Mode**:
   - Click the **COMPARE** folder tab.
   - Click **"Try Sample Comparison (v1 vs v2)"**.
   - Click **"Tracing Paper View 📑"** and drag the split slider.

3. **Test Navigate Mode**:
   - Click the **NAVIGATE** folder tab.
   - Click **"📅 Add to Calendar (.ics)"** — notice `document-deadlines.ics` downloads instantly.
   - Click **"💬 Copy WhatsApp Summary"** — paste into any text editor to verify formatting.

4. **Test Multilingual & Accessibility**:
   - Change language to **हिन्दी (Hindi)** or **ಕನ್ನಡ (Kannada)** in the top right.
   - Click **"Aa"** to test the dyslexia-friendly font.
   - Press **`?`** on your keyboard to reveal the shortcuts modal.
   - Toggle **"Night"** to view the dark mahogany desk theme.
   - Click **"Forget everything"** to verify complete memory reset.
