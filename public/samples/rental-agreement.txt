RESIDENTIAL TENANCY AGREEMENT

This Residential Tenancy Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on 1st March 2024 between:

Landlord: Priya Sharma, residing at 42 Greenpark Colony, Bengaluru 560029
Tenant: Arjun Mehta, currently residing at 15 BTM Layout, Bengaluru 560076

1. PROPERTY
The Landlord agrees to rent to the Tenant the residential premises located at Flat No. 3B, Sunrise Apartments, 78 Indiranagar Main Road, Bengaluru 560038 (the "Property") for residential purposes only.

2. TERM
The tenancy shall commence on 1st March 2024 and shall continue for a period of 11 (eleven) months, ending on 31st January 2025, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Agreement. This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive 11-month periods unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least 60 (sixty) days before the expiry of the then-current term.

3. RENT
The monthly rent payable by the Tenant is Rs. 28,000 (Rupees Twenty-Eight Thousand only), payable on or before the 5th day of each calendar month. If rent is not paid by the 10th of the month, a late fee of Rs. 500 per day shall be charged until payment is made. If rent remains unpaid for more than 30 days, the Landlord may immediately terminate this Agreement without further notice.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT
The Tenant has paid a refundable security deposit of Rs. 1,68,000 (equivalent to six months' rent) upon signing this Agreement. The deposit shall be refunded within 30 days of the Tenant vacating the Property, less any deductions for damages beyond normal wear and tear, unpaid rent, or cleaning costs. The Landlord is not obligated to pay interest on the deposit.

5. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
The Tenant shall be responsible for all repairs costing less than Rs. 2,000 arising from normal use. The Landlord shall be responsible for major structural repairs and maintenance of plumbing, electrical systems, and common areas. The Tenant shall not carry out any structural alterations without prior written consent from the Landlord. Any alterations approved by the Landlord shall become the property of the Landlord upon termination of this Agreement.

6. UTILITIES
The Tenant shall pay all utility charges including electricity, gas, water, and internet. The Tenant shall pay maintenance charges of Rs. 3,500 per month directly to the apartment society.

7. LANDLORD ENTRY
The Landlord or their representative may enter the Property at any time and without prior notice for inspection, repairs, or any other purpose deemed necessary by the Landlord.

8. SUBLETTING
The Tenant shall not sublet, assign, or transfer this Agreement or any interest herein to any third party without the prior written consent of the Landlord, which may be withheld in the Landlord's sole discretion.

9. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving 2 (two) months' written notice. The Tenant may not terminate this Agreement in the first 4 months of the tenancy without forfeiting the entire security deposit. If the Tenant vacates without notice, the entire security deposit shall be forfeited.

10. PETS AND GUESTS
No pets are permitted in the Property under any circumstances. Guests may not stay for more than 3 consecutive nights without the Landlord's written approval. Violation of this clause shall be grounds for immediate termination.

11. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any disputes arising from this Agreement shall be resolved through arbitration in Bengaluru, Karnataka, under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. The arbitrator shall be appointed solely by the Landlord. Costs of arbitration shall be borne equally by both parties.

12. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of Karnataka, India.

13. ENTIRE AGREEMENT
This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior understandings. Any modifications must be in writing and signed by both parties.
