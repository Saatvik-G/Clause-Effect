RESIDENTIAL TENANCY AGREEMENT — VERSION 2

This Residential Tenancy Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on 1st March 2024 between:

Landlord: Priya Sharma, residing at 42 Greenpark Colony, Bengaluru 560029
Tenant: Arjun Mehta, currently residing at 15 BTM Layout, Bengaluru 560076

1. PROPERTY
The Landlord agrees to rent to the Tenant the residential premises located at Flat No. 3B, Sunrise Apartments, 78 Indiranagar Main Road, Bengaluru 560038 for residential purposes only.

2. TERM
The tenancy shall commence on 1st March 2024 and continue for 11 months, ending on 31st January 2025. This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive 11-month periods unless either party provides 30 (thirty) days' written notice of non-renewal before expiry.

3. RENT
Monthly rent: Rs. 31,000 (Rupees Thirty-One Thousand only), payable by the 5th of each month. If unpaid by the 15th of the month, a late fee of Rs. 200 per day applies. If rent remains unpaid for 45 days, the Landlord may initiate termination proceedings with 7 days' notice.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT
The Tenant has paid a refundable security deposit of Rs. 62,000 (equivalent to two months' rent). The deposit shall be refunded within 15 days of the Tenant vacating, less deductions for damages beyond normal wear and tear. The Landlord shall pay simple interest at 6% per annum on the deposit.

5. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
The Tenant shall be responsible for repairs costing less than Rs. 1,000. The Landlord shall handle major repairs within 7 days of written notice. The Tenant may carry out minor cosmetic changes without prior approval.

6. UTILITIES
The Tenant shall pay electricity and internet. The Landlord shall pay water and maintenance charges.

7. LANDLORD ENTRY
The Landlord shall give at least 48 (forty-eight) hours' written notice before entering the Property, except in genuine emergencies.

8. SUBLETTING
Subletting is permitted with the Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

9. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving 30 (thirty) days' written notice after the first 2 months of tenancy. The security deposit shall not be forfeited for proper notice.

10. PETS
One small pet (under 10 kg) is permitted with a refundable pet deposit of Rs. 5,000.

11. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Disputes shall first be attempted through mediation. If unresolved within 30 days, either party may approach the Bangalore Rent Authority or civil courts.

12. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of Karnataka, India.
