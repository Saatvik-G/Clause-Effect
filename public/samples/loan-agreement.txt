1. LOAN AMOUNT AND DISBURSEMENT
Loan Amount: Rs. 5,00,000 (Rupees Five Lakhs only)
Disbursement: Amount shall be disbursed to the Borrower's bank account within 2 working days of signing this Agreement and completing verification.

2. INTEREST RATE
The loan shall carry interest at 24% per annum (2% per month), calculated on the reducing balance method. The interest rate is variable and the Lender may revise it with 30 days' notice. If the revised rate is not acceptable, the Borrower must repay the outstanding amount within 15 days of notice.

3. REPAYMENT SCHEDULE
The loan shall be repaid in 24 equal monthly instalments of Rs. 25,840 each. The first instalment is due on 5th April 2024, and subsequent instalments are due on the 5th of each month. The Lender will deduct EMIs via NACH mandate from the Borrower's bank account.

4. PROCESSING FEE
A processing fee of 3% of the loan amount (Rs. 15,000) shall be deducted from the disbursed amount. This fee is non-refundable under any circumstances.

5. PREPAYMENT
The Borrower may prepay the entire outstanding amount after 6 months from disbursement, subject to a prepayment penalty of 5% of the outstanding principal at the time of prepayment. Partial prepayments are not permitted.

6. LATE PAYMENT
If any instalment is delayed beyond the due date, a late payment charge of 3% per month shall apply on the overdue amount for the period of delay. Additionally, the Lender shall report the delay to all credit bureaus.

7. DEFAULT
The Borrower shall be in default if: (a) any instalment is unpaid for more than 7 days; (b) the Borrower provides false information; (c) the Borrower is subject to insolvency proceedings; or (d) the Lender determines in its sole judgment that the Borrower's financial condition has materially deteriorated.

8. ACCELERATION
Upon default, the entire outstanding loan amount, including interest, penalties, and all fees, shall become immediately due and payable without any further notice. The Lender may take any legal action to recover the outstanding amount including selling the pledged assets.

9. SECURITY
The Borrower pledges as security: (a) Post-dated cheques for all 24 EMIs; (b) A personal guarantee from Meena Verma (spouse). The Lender may encash the cheques or invoke the guarantee upon default.

10. ARBITRATION
All disputes shall be resolved by a sole arbitrator appointed exclusively by the Lender in Bengaluru. The Borrower waives the right to approach any court for any relief connected to this Agreement. All arbitration costs shall be borne by the Borrower.

11. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. Jurisdiction: Bengaluru courts only.
